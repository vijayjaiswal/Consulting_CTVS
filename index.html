Essential care areas

Support across the cardiovascular care continuum.

Consulting CTVS is designed for patients and families who need calm, practical guidance after diagnosis, procedure, discharge, or chronic cardiac illness.

Specialized Monitoring

Early recognition of chest pain, breathlessness, palpitations, daily weight changes, and other warning signs that need action.

Acute Intervention Readiness

Family preparedness for emergency response, CPR awareness, AED use, and escalation pathways during cardiac distress.

Medication Confidence

Clear education on antiplatelets, nitrates, anticoagulants, doses, timing, side effects, and adherence routines.

Post-Procedure Care

Guidance after angioplasty, stenting, pacemaker insertion, wound checks, groin-site observation, and pulse monitoring.

Rehabilitation Support

Structured recovery goals, gradual activity planning, fatigue management, anxiety support, and heart-healthy lifestyle coaching.

Family Education

Practical home-care routines so families can support diet, smoking cessation, follow-up, symptom tracking, and emotional recovery.

Patient programs

Clinical reassurance without making care feel heavy.

Each consultation is structured around the patient's current phase: hospital discharge, procedure recovery, chronic heart failure management, or rehabilitation readiness.

01Assess

Review symptoms, procedure history, current medication, risk signals, and family concerns.

02Plan

Create a practical monitoring, diet, medication, follow-up, and activity routine.

03Coach

Support lifestyle change, anxiety management, rehabilitation adherence, and doctor escalation decisions.

When patients often book

  • After angioplasty, stenting, or pacemaker insertion
  • Before discharge from hospital to home care
  • For heart failure monitoring and readmission prevention
  • When family members need clear care instructions
  • During cardiac rehabilitation and lifestyle transition
